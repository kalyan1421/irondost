# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-05

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** IronDost picks up clothes in Hyderabad, irons or washes them, and delivers them back to the customer.

**What it does:** Customers choose ironing, wash & iron or dry cleaning, add items, select pickup and delivery windows, confirm a server-calculated quote, pay online or by cash when enabled, and track the order.

**Product category:** Doorstep laundry, ironing and dry-cleaning service.

**Product type:** Local service marketplace/operations platform with a customer mobile app, admin console, workshop processing and delivery partners.

**Business model:** Per-item and/or per-kg service pricing with pickup/delivery charges or minimum-order rules, seasonal promotions, repeat-order bundles and potential B2B recurring routes. Final launch pricing is still an open decision; seed catalogue prices are placeholders.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Initially households and apartment communities around LB Nagar and east Hyderabad; later PGs, salons, clinics, offices and other small businesses with recurring garment/linen needs.

**Decision-makers:** Household buyer, working professional, apartment resident champion, PG/office manager and small-business owner.

**Primary use case:** Remove the time and uncertainty involved in washing and ironing everyday clothes while giving customers a clear price, pickup window, return window and order status.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Get office, school and everyday clothes cleaned or pressed without spending an evening on laundry.
- Prepare festival and occasion garments without visiting a dry cleaner.
- Repeat a reliable pickup routine with minimal follow-up.

**Use cases:** Weekly wash & iron, urgent ironing, festival preparation, bedsheets/household items, dry cleaning, apartment pickup routes and recurring small-business loads.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Customers want clean, crisp clothes without the time, effort and uncertainty of doing everything themselves or relying on an informal provider.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Home washing and ironing consume time and attention.
- Local informal providers may not show prices, status or recovery steps clearly.
- Large chains can feel expensive, generic or distant from the local neighbourhood.
- Public competitor pages frequently make area, price and delivery promises difficult to compare.

**What it costs them:** Time, missed routines, last-minute festival stress, repeated phone follow-ups and risk around delicate garments or late delivery.

**Emotional tension:** “I need this to come back on time and in the same condition.”

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** LaundryGo, Fabrico, Pearlclean, Tumbledry, UClean, DhobiLite and local LB Nagar laundry/dry-cleaning providers.

**Secondary:** Home washing, neighbourhood ironing vendors, apartment dhobis and self-service laundromats.

**Indirect:** Buying additional clothes instead of cleaning them, delaying wear, or asking household help/family to handle the load.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Hyperlocal LB Nagar launch and pickup density.
- Clear itemised prices and service units before confirmation.
- Pickup/delivery window selection and order tracking.
- App plus WhatsApp fallback for low-friction booking.
- Process visibility and support that match the actual operating promise.

**How we do it differently:** Start with a small service area, validate a reliable route, publish the real rate card and use the app to reduce follow-up rather than over-promising citywide scale.

**Why that is better:** Customers can judge price, timing and accountability before handing over clothes.

**Why customers choose us:** Less effort than home laundry, more clarity than an informal provider and more local relevance than a national chain.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “Will you serve my exact address?” | Show serviceability before booking and state the active neighbourhoods clearly. |
| “Will the price change after pickup?” | Show units and price before confirmation; if inspection can change a quote, explain it and require approval. |
| “What if clothes are late or damaged?” | Publish a real support, escalation and refund/compensation policy. |
| “Why not use my local ironing person?” | Lead with pickup convenience, tracking and consistent booking; do not attack local providers. |
| “Is the app live?” | Send ads only to the production app or provide a working WhatsApp fallback. |

**Anti-persona:** Customers outside the active service radius, one-off highly specialised garment restoration until capability is verified, and price-only shoppers whose orders cannot support pickup and processing costs.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Laundry takes too long, local service is opaque, or the customer needs a dependable festival/office turnaround.

**Pull:** Doorstep convenience, clear booking, visible status and a local Dasara offer.

**Habit:** Existing dhobi, household help, home washing and reluctance to trust a new provider with clothes.

**Anxiety:** Damage, loss, late delivery, hidden fees and poor support.

## Customer Language

**How they describe the problem:**

- “Laundry ka tension.”
- “No time to wash and iron after work.”
- “Will you pick up from my area?”
- “When will I get my clothes back?”

**How they describe the solution:**

- “Pickup and delivery laundry.”
- “Wash and iron.”
- “Pressing at home pickup.”

**Words to use:** doorstep, clear price, pickup slot, return window, crisp, everyday laundry, LB Nagar, trackable, wash & iron.

**Words to avoid until proven:** cheapest, fastest in Hyderabad, guaranteed stain removal, zero damage, same-day, free, premium, 100% safe.

**Glossary:**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ironing | Pressing service priced by item where configured |
| Wash & iron | Washing and finishing service for everyday garments |
| Dry cleaning | Specialised garment-care service; use only for enabled items |
| Serviceability | Whether the customer address is inside the current operating area |
| Return window | Confirmed delivery date/time window shown before booking |

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Helpful, local, direct and reassuring.

**Style:** Short sentences, plain language, English with carefully reviewed Telugu/Hinglish variants. Explain the service rather than using generic luxury language.

**Personality:** Dependable, transparent, practical and warm.

## Proof Points

**Metrics:** To be collected after pilot: on-time pickup, on-time delivery, repeat-order rate, complaint rate, average order value and first-order CAC.

**Customers:** Initial controlled pilot customers in LB Nagar; no public customer logos yet.

**Testimonials:** Do not publish until permission is recorded and the order is completed.

**Value themes:**

| Theme | Proof |
|---|---|
| Convenience | Pickup/delivery booking flow and real route coverage |
| Transparency | Server quote, itemised basket and status timeline |
| Local reliability | Pilot results and apartment-route performance |

## Goals

**Business goal:** Launch successfully around LB Nagar on Dasara 2026, prove reliable unit economics and build a repeat customer base before expanding the service area.

**Conversion action:** Book and complete a first pickup through the production app; use Click-to-WhatsApp as the fallback.

**Current metrics:** Not yet established. The first 10–20 controlled orders are the baseline.
