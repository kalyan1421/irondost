Google Business Profile

Without a profile, IronDost cannot appear in the Maps pack at all

"Ironing near me" and "laundry pickup LB Nagar" are decided in Maps. No profile, no JustDial or Sulekha listing, no brand result exists today. Create it as a service-area business with the address hidden.

Setup steps

  1. Deploy irondost.in first. The profile's website link should never point at a parked page; leave it empty until then.
  2. Create the profile with the owner's Google account plus a second manager. Check first that none exists under Cloud Ironing Factory; claim it if so.
  3. Verify by live video in one continuous take: the street and a landmark, the pressing table and packed orders, something branded, and proof of management. Use the real work address. 79934 34932 must answer.
  4. Fill every field below, add 10+ real photos (no generated images), then 2–3 new photos a week for a month.
  5. Post weekly and ask every delivered customer for an honest review. Reply to every review within two days.
  6. Measure after 30 days: a 3×3 rank grid around LB Nagar Circle for "laundry service" and "ironing service", plus calls and website clicks.

Ten hooks

  1. Curiosity: one screen most laundry services would rather you never saw: the total, before anyone touches your clothes.
  2. Loss: if ironing takes you two hours every Sunday, that is more than four full days a year.
  3. Contrast: most people expect the bill after the clothes are gone. Here it comes before.
  4. Specificity (strongest): Shirt ₹15. Trousers ₹15. Saree ₹50. Windows 7–11, 11–4, 4–8.
  5. Controversy: ironing your own shirts is not a virtue.
  6. Pattern interrupt: …and that is the chair. The one with all the clothes on it.
  7. Identity: for the LB Nagar parent still ironing uniforms at 10 pm.
  8. Social proof: an ironing cart you trust, at your door with a printed price.
  9. Future pace: Monday, 8 am. The shirt is already pressed.
  10. Simplicity: We pick up. We press. We bring it back.
