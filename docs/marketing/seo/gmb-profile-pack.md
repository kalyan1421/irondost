- Google picks the method. For new service-area businesses in India it is usually **live video verification** in the app.
- Record in one continuous take, no editing: the street and a landmark near the address, the work area (pressing table, irons, packed orders), something branded (the IronDost bag, a printed poster, the app open), and proof you manage the business (opening the admin on a phone, a shop licence or GST certificate if you have one).
- The address must be where the work really happens. No virtual offices, and not a friend's shop.
- Phone 79934 34932 must answer, because Google may call it.
- After verifying, avoid big edits (name, category, address) for a few weeks, since they can trigger re-verification.
