## What to do next

1. **Deploy irondost.in** (the profile's website link should not point at a parked page).
2. **Create the Business Profile** with the owner's Google account: service-area business, address hidden, categories and services from the pack.
3. **Verify.** Expect video verification. Film in one continuous take: the street and a nearby landmark, the work area (pressing table, iron, packed orders), the branded bag or poster, and proof of management (opening the admin or the Business Profile dashboard). Use the real address where the work happens. A virtual office or a home without any business sign risks suspension. Do not use someone else's shop address.
4. **Fill the profile:** 10+ real photos (no generated images), services, hours, booking link (the app or WhatsApp).
5. **Reviews:** ask every delivered customer with the template in the pack. No discounts for reviews, no filtering out the unhappy ones. Reply to every review within two days.
6. **Citations:** JustDial, Sulekha, Bing Places (can import from Google), Apple Business Connect, and the Facebook page. Identical name, phone and website everywhere.
7. **Website:** the `/areas` page with the PIN codes, and `LocalBusiness` schema with `areaServed` set to the localities, a `GeoCircle` of 6 km around LB Nagar Circle, and `sameAs` pointing to the profile once it has a public URL.
8. **Measure** after 30 days: a 3×3 grid for the two main keywords (about 18 paid searches), profile calls, website clicks and direction requests.
