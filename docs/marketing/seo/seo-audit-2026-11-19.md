# IronDost SEO audit

Checked 19 November 2026. Covers the live site **https://irondost-app.web.app** and the local source in `apps/web`, which already contains changes not yet deployed: the new store links, phone 79934 34932, and five `/blog` guides. No SEO tool (Ahrefs, Semrush, Search Console) is connected, so the search-demand and difficulty columns are judgement calls from what customers ask and what currently ranks, not measured volumes.

## Executive summary

The technical base is strong: a static Next.js site, fast responses from the Hyderabad cache (0.2 to 0.4 s), HTTPS with HSTS, one H1 per page, alt text on every image, a sitemap, `robots.txt` and LocalBusiness (`DryCleaningOrLaundry`) schema. **The live site is wrong about the business, though.** It has the old phone number (90632 90012), the old "cloud ironing" Play listing, no App Store link, and it says "across Hyderabad" when the service covers 6 km around LB Nagar. The word "LB Nagar" never appears on it. It also lives on a `web.app` address that will not build lasting authority. Nobody ranks for "IronDost" yet, and a search for the brand returns nothing.

**Top three priorities:**

1. Deploy the local site on **irondost.in** with every canonical, sitemap and schema URL on that domain, and redirect `web.app`, irondost.com and irondost.shop to it.
2. Make the pages local: name LB Nagar and the 12 localities, and fix `areaServed` from "City: Hyderabad" to the real areas.
3. Publish the five guides, then a `/prices` page and an areas page, and connect Search Console.

**Overall:** good foundation, critical content and domain issues. All three are quick to fix.

## Prioritised action plan

**Quick wins (this week)**

1. **Deploy the current source on irondost.in** (new phone, store links, guides) with `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://irondost.in`. Impact high; 1 to 2 hours plus DNS. Depends on DNS at Hostinger.
2. **301 the other hosts** (www, irondost.com, irondost.shop once bought, `web.app` after the store URLs change) to irondost.in. Impact high; 1 hour.
3. **Make the home page local:** title, meta, an "around LB Nagar" H2, the 12 localities, and schema `areaServed` plus `GeoCircle`. Impact high; 1 to 2 hours.
4. **Search Console:** verify irondost.in by DNS TXT, submit the sitemap, request indexing for home, `/blog` and the 5 guides. Impact high; 30 minutes.
5. **Google Business Profile:** create and verify (see `gmb-profile-pack.md`), with the website link to irondost.in. Impact very high for "near me"; 1 hour plus verification.

**Strategic (this quarter)**

1. `/prices` after the owner confirms prices, then `/areas` and the three service pages. High impact; half a day each.
2. "Our work" gallery with real photos, reused on the Business Profile and in the redesign. High impact; needs a shoot.
3. Reviews: ask every delivered customer and reply to all of them. Reviews drive the Maps pack. High impact; ongoing.
4. Citations: JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART and Bing Places with exactly the same name, phone and website. Medium impact; half a day.
5. Two guides a month from the gaps above, each answering one question in its first sentence. Medium impact; ongoing.
6. Links: local Hyderabad blogs, apartment-association newsletters, PG directories. Medium impact; ongoing.
