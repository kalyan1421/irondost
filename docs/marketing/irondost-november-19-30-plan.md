- Objective: messages. Audience: people living within the smallest radius around LB Nagar Circle that covers the served PINs.
- Two creatives, same text: `reel-01-price`, `reel-02-pile`.
- Text: "LB Nagar, ironing piling up? IronDost picks up from your door. Shirt ironing ₹15. See the price and the return slot before you confirm."
- Headline: "Laundry pickup near you". Button: Send WhatsApp message.
- Budget: ₹250 a day, ₹1,750 in all. Stop a creative that spends ₹600 with no serviceable chat.
- Judge on delivered first orders, not clicks.

## WhatsApp replies

- **First reply:** "Hi! Thanks for contacting IronDost. Which area are you in, and what do you need: ironing, wash and iron, or dry cleaning?"
- **Area yes:** "We pick up there. Shirt ironing is ₹15, trousers ₹15. Shall I send the full rate card? You can book in the app, or tell me the clothes and I will book it for you."
- **Area no:** "Sorry, we are not in that area yet. If you enter your address in the app and tap 'Tell me when you're here', we will let you know when we reach you."
- **After delivery:** "Did everything come back right? If anything is off, tell me and we will fix it today."
