## What to take from them

1. **Keep the Season 1 concept.** Same story, same cast, same hook and cliffhanger for every episode. The references change *how* we make and post the clips, not what the series is about.
2. **Big, physical hooks shot like an ad (from #1).** The Tide reel stops the scroll with one huge physical moment in the first second. Season 1's hooks already work this way (the almirah avalanche, the 7:02 doorbell, the falling ring, the burning iron, the table slam), so each C1 opens mid-action on that moment and is lit and framed like a premium commercial.
3. **Commercial-grade prompts (from #1 and #2).** One 10-second prompt per clip, written as: style → characters → a product-consistency paragraph for the IronDost look → beats second by second → camera → dialogue → sound → first and last frame → what to attach → negative prompt.
4. **Show the making (from #1 and #2).** Their biggest numbers came from posting the finished ad *with* the storyboard and the prompt, then "Comment WORD and I'll DM you the prompt". 5,583 comments on a 13K account. We do the same with a second post per episode.
5. **Kinetic captions (from #3).** Word-by-word white captions in the edit, Telugu line on top and English below, in the middle third of the screen. They keep people watching with the sound off.
6. **Seedance for 10-second clips (from #1 and #4).** Both creators used Seedance. It makes 10-second clips with reference images, which fits "every prompt covers 10 seconds". Veo also works but stops at 8 seconds, so you would extend once.

## Plan for the series

- **Everything is AI.** No screen recordings, no real footage. Phones always face away from the camera; the app moment is shown as a royal-blue glow from the phone on the actor's face. Bills, prices and the logo are text added in the edit, never drawn by the AI.
- **4 clips of 10 seconds per episode, 40 seconds in all,** then a 4-second end card laid over the last frame. 40 clips for the season.
- **The cast stays as Season 1:** Ravi, Bunty, Dost (Srinu); Amma and Divya are only voices, or seen from behind.
- **Hooks are protected at two levels.** Every episode keeps its original first-second hook, on-screen hook text and cliffhanger. The season keeps its spine: 10 days to Divya's engagement, the lucky shirt and the ring, ending on the taglines and the Season 2 tease.
- **Idea parked for Season 2:** a lucky shirt with a stitched face that reacts (from reel #4) and a monster made of dirty clothes (from reel #1). Test images are in `videos/dost-undi-ga/refs/not-used/`.
