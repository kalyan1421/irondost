# Dost Undi Ga! Season 1 · v3 · all-AI, 10-second prompts
IronDost reel series · 10 episodes · 40 clips of 10 seconds · every shot AI-generated · rewritten 23 Nov 2026 after studying four reference reels (see the research and reach plan).

**Battala tension? IronDost undi ga!** · **Kapdon ki tension? Dost hai na!**

## What changed from v2
- **Every clip is 10 seconds and AI.** 4 clips per episode, 40 seconds, plus a 4-second end card laid over the last frame. No screen recordings or real footage anywhere.
- **Same Season 1 concept.** Same story, same cast (Ravi, Bunty, Dost; Amma and Divya as voices), same hook, hook text and cliffhanger for every episode. Only the shots changed: every one is now AI.
- **Commercial-grade prompt layout** (from the Surf Excel reel): style, cast, a brand-consistency paragraph, a timeline that fills all 10 seconds, camera, timed dialogue, sound, first and last frame, attach list.
- **A much stronger negative prompt** on every clip: the clip's own risks first, then 7 groups (faces, people, speech, text, continuity, image quality, content) covering every failure seen in the INDYUG audit.
- **The app without a screen.** Phones always face away; the app is a royal-blue glow. All writing (prices, bills, codes, countdowns, captions) is added in the edit.

## Which tool
| Tool | Use it for | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Seedance 2.0** (through invideo, Dreamina or Higgsfield) | First choice. Both big reference creators used it. 10-second clips with reference images | Reference-to-video, 9:16, 1080p, 10 s, audio on |
| **Kling 2.x** | Second choice if Seedance mangles a face | Elements / multi-image reference, 9:16, 10 s, professional mode |
| **Google Flow (Veo)** | Best Telugu lip-sync, but only 8 s | Ingredients to Video (3 images), then Extend once with the same prompt's last two beats |

## How to make one clip
1. Upload the images in the clip's **Attach** line (the first three if your tool only takes three).
2. Paste the **whole prompt**. If the tool has a separate negative box, paste the **negative prompt** there too.
3. Generate 2–3 takes. Keep a take only if: faces match, bags and polo are blank royal blue, each line is said once by the right lips, no music, no AI-drawn text.
4. If the voice sounds wrong, keep the picture and dub the line with a real voice.
5. Save as `videos/dost-undi-ga/epNN/E{n}-C{k}.mp4`. In CapCut: word-by-word captions (Telugu on top, English below), the hook text, the logo on the polo and bags, then the end card.
