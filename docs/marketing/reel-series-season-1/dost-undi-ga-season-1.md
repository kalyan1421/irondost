## 3. How to make each episode with AI (free-tool route)

1. **Keyframes:** for each shot in the script, generate a still with the character reference image attached plus the shot description and the style line.
2. **Animate:** turn each still into a 3–5 second clip with an image-to-video tool. Keep movements small (a turn, a reaction, a hand movement). Big actions look fake.
3. **Voices:** **record the dialogue with real people** (you, friends, staff) on a phone in a quiet room. AI voices still sound wrong for Telugu and Dakhni accents, and a real accent is what makes local viewers trust it. Then lip-sync the clips to the recordings, or keep the speaker off-face (back, over-the-shoulder, phone shot) where lip-sync fails.
4. **Edit (CapCut or similar, free):** cut to the beat, add burned-in **English subtitles** plus Telugu where it helps, sound effects, music, and the end card.
5. **Real app screens:** screen-record the real app on a phone for every app moment. Never generate the app with AI.

**Budget warning.** Free tiers of AI video tools give only a few clips a day. One episode is about 8–12 clips, so 10 episodes is roughly 100 clips. With no budget, start production now and expect it to take a few weeks for all 10, or plan one paid month of a single video tool. Have Episodes 1–5 finished before Episode 1 goes live.

**Disclose AI.** YouTube requires you to turn on the "altered or synthetic content" label for realistic AI people. Instagram and Facebook show an "AI info" label; turn it on yourself. Also put "AI-made story" in small text on the end card. Hiding it risks removal and loses trust.

## 6. When and where to post

### Research summary (no budget, so organic timing matters)
Studies disagree, and most are not India-specific, but the most common recommendations for India are:
- **Instagram Reels:** weekday evenings around **7:30–8:30 pm IST**; some studies also show a **2–5 pm** weekday window.
- **YouTube Shorts:** **7–9 pm IST** in most guides; some also show 7–9 am.
- **Facebook:** no reliable India data; general guides point to weekday mornings.
- Every source agrees: after two weeks, **your own Insights beat any study**.

### The schedule

| Platform | When | How (free) |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reel | **Every day 7:30 pm** | Schedule in Meta Business Suite |
| Facebook Reel | Same time, 7:30 pm | Cross-post from Meta Business Suite |
| YouTube Short | Same day, **8:00 pm** | Schedule in YouTube Studio; add to a playlist "Dost Undi Ga — Season 1" |
| WhatsApp Status | **Next morning 8:00 am** (people check status with morning tea) | Post from the business number; trim to the hook + story, add the end card |
| WhatsApp Channel / broadcast | Next morning 8:30 am | Short line + YouTube link so people can watch from Ep 1 |

**Why the same time every day:** with a series, viewers start expecting it ("7:30 ki next episode"). Say it on the end card: **"Prati roju 7:30 PM ki next episode."**

**Start day:** start on a **Monday** so Episodes 9 and 10 (the climax) land on Tuesday–Wednesday of the second week and the finale gets a full weekday. Only start once **Episodes 1–5 are fully edited**.

**After 14 days:** open Instagram Insights → "Most active times" and YouTube Studio → "When your viewers are on YouTube". If your audience peaks at a different hour, move the slot there for Season 2.

### Free reach boosters
1. **Pin Episode 1** to the top of Instagram and Facebook during the season, and add "▶️ Ep 1 here" to the bio.
2. **Reply to every comment within the first hour**: this pushes the reel further in the algorithm. Use comments in the story ("Bunty ni kshaminchala?" → answer in Ep 8).
3. **Collab posts:** invite 1–2 local Hyderabad / LB Nagar pages or food bloggers as Instagram collaborators on Ep 1 and Ep 10 (free if they like it).
4. **Location tag** every reel: LB Nagar / Dilsukhnagar / Nagole.
5. **Telugu trending audio** at low volume under the dialogue, where the platform allows it.
6. **Ask existing customers** to share the WhatsApp Status. The people who already trust you are the best channel.

### What to measure per episode
- **3-second hold** (Instagram: "skip rate"; YouTube: "viewed vs swiped away") — if under about 60%, the hook is too slow.
- **Average watch %** — the cliffhanger is working if it is high.
- **Follows and profile visits** from the reel.
- **Calls / WhatsApps and app installs** in the week (ask new customers "Reel chusara?").
