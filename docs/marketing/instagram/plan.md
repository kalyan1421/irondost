# Instagram plan: 19 November to 31 December 2026

Six weeks, 4 to 5 posts a week, at least 3 reels. For 19 to 30 November the [day-by-day plan](../irondost-november-19-30-plan.md) is the one to follow; it posts more often in the first fortnight than the outline below. Every asset named here is in [december-2026-pack](../december-2026-pack/README.md); captions are in that README. Nothing is scheduled or posted: this is a plan for you to run.

The hour matters far less than the first two seconds of the reel. Times below are a starting point for a household audience in Hyderabad (evening, after work), not a tested result.

## Stories, every day

3 to 5 frames: slots open today, one price, one "DM your area" frame. Question box on Wednesdays. Save answers to highlights: Prices, How it works, Areas, FAQ.

## The engagement round (20 minutes a day, before posting)

Build the list by looking, not guessing; I have not checked which accounts exist.

- **5 reach:** east-Hyderabad pages people already follow (LB Nagar, Dilsukhnagar, Vanasthalipuram and Nagole community and food pages, a Hyderabad events page). Comment early and usefully.
- **3 peers:** small local service businesses in the same circle (a tailor or boutique, a home-food service, a salon). They reciprocate, and they can become partner counters.
- **2 buyers:** apartment-association or gated-community pages, and a PG or hostel page in Dilsukhnagar. Comment for weeks before any message; never pitch in a comment.
