## Messages for operators

**First reply:** “Hi! Thanks for contacting IronDost. Please share your locality or apartment, the service you need, and roughly how many items. We’ll check availability, prices and pickup slots.”

**Quote:** “For [ITEMS AND SERVICE], the estimated price is [AMOUNT], including [FEES OR MINIMUM]. Available pickup: [WINDOW]. Expected return: [WINDOW]. Would you like to confirm? We’ll confirm any change before proceeding.”

**Apartment manager:** “Hello [NAME], we’re IronDost, a laundry and ironing pickup service serving supported parts of LB Nagar. Would it be okay to share one short introduction and booking link with interested residents? We can first confirm if your community is in our service area.”

**After delivery:** “Hi [NAME], did your order arrive as expected? Please let us know if anything needs attention.” Ask for an honest review only after the service is delivered; do not offer a reward for a positive review. Request consent before using the review in a post.

## End-of-week scorecard

| Metric | Record | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified, serviceable chats | Count by post/ad/source | Fix audience or address targeting if many leads are outside the service area. |
| Confirmed orders | Count and track cancellations | If many quotes do not convert, inspect price, slots and response speed. |
| Delivered first orders | Count only after delivery | Compare with ad spend and capacity; pending bookings are not completed customers. |
| Ad cost per delivered new customer | Attributed ad spend ÷ delivered new customers | Compare with contribution margin and the founder's agreed payback; do not invent a CAC target. |
| Pickup and delivery reliability | On-time events ÷ total events | Pause or limit promotion if fulfilment cannot keep up. |
| Complaints | Count by type and resolve individually | Fix service problems before seeking more volume. |

Carry late-week orders into the next report when they are delivered. A seven-day campaign is too short to assess 30-day repeat behaviour.
