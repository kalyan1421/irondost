#### Ten hooks

1. **Curiosity**: one screen most laundry services would rather you never saw: the total, before anyone touches your clothes.
2. **Loss**: if ironing takes you two hours every Sunday, that is more than four full days a year.
3. **Contrast**: most people expect the bill after the clothes are gone. Here it comes before.
