## Dost Undi Ga!

Battala tension? IronDost undi ga! · Kapdon ki tension? Dost hai na!

Ten daily episodes of 40–45 seconds for Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and WhatsApp. Every clip below has a full **keyframe (image) prompt** and a **motion (video) prompt**, ready to copy. Clips marked Real are screen recordings of the real app or edit graphics, so no AI is needed for them.

[Laundry 3 days] [Ironing 2 days] [Express 6 hrs · 2× price] [₹500+ → 10% off · DOST10] [Call / WhatsApp 79934 34932 · 70329 90632] [Post daily 7:30 pm IST]

Start here

### How to make one clip

#### 1 · Keyframe

Paste the **keyframe prompt** into the image tool **with the character reference images attached** (the ones named in the prompt). One image per clip. Keep the image only if the face matches the reference.

#### 2 · Motion

Upload that keyframe to the image-to-video tool and paste the **motion prompt**. Generate 2–3 takes, keep the best. Movement stays small, because big actions break faces.

#### 3 · Voice

Record the dialogue with real people on a phone (Telugu and Hyderabadi accents sound fake from AI). Lip-sync, or keep the speaker off-face where the script says so.

#### 4 · Edit

Cut in CapCut: on-screen text from each clip, English subtitles, SFX, music, logo on Dost's polo and bags, then the end card. Turn on the AI-content label when posting.

#### Rules that stop clips going missing or breaking

- Make every clip in the list, in order. Clip IDs (E3-C5) match the edit timeline, so a missing ID is easy to spot.
- Generate all keyframes of one episode in the same session with the same references, before animating any of them.
- Never let the AI draw text, phone screens or the logo. Those are added in the edit (the prompts already say "no text").
- The navy-blue checked "lucky shirt", red velvet ring box and maroon Kanchi pattu saree are story props. Generate the prop sheet once and attach it whenever a prop appears.
- Ep 4 machines must look like **your** unit: attach 3–4 photos of your real washers, dryers and steam press as references.

The first 3 seconds

### Hook rules for every episode

#### Frame 1 already moves

No logo, no "Episode 3", no slow fade. The very first frame is mid-action: an avalanche, a falling ring, a burning iron.

#### Sound hits at 0.0 s

A doorbell, a slam, a sizzle or a scream on the first frame. Many people scroll with the sound on low, and a hit makes them stop.

#### Text on screen by 0.5 s

A short, bold line with a number or stakes ("10 rojullo engagement. Clean battalu: 0"). Big, centred in the top third, two lines max.

#### Open a question

Every hook raises a question that only the rest of the reel answers. Every ending raises the next one. The hook formula used is named on each episode.
