The logo

## Wash, iron, deliver, in one picture.

IronDost picks up clothes, washes and presses them, and brings them back to the door. The mark shows the whole job at a glance, and the name adds the promise: a *dost*, a friend who takes care of it.

![IronDost logo: a washing machine with a steam iron in front of it and soap bubbles, next to the word IronDost and the tagline Wash · Iron · Deliver](image omitted)

#### Front-load washer

Washing. The porthole shows water mid-cycle, so it reads as laundry, not an appliance shop.

#### Steam iron

Pressing, the service people book most. It sits in front of the washer and carries the royal blue of "Dost".

#### Soap bubbles

Freshness, rising above the iron. They drop out of the small mark, where they would only add noise.

#### Wordmark

Text-only places: email signatures, receipts, partner listings.

Colourway notes (from the page's colourway switcher):

- **Colour:** for white and light backgrounds.
- **Reverse:** for ink and other dark backgrounds. The colour mark already works on dark, so the mark versions stay the same.
- **One colour, ink:** single-colour printing, stamps, rubber seals and receipts.
- **One colour, white:** on royal, on photos, and for embroidery or screen printing on dark bags.

Space and size

### Give it room, keep it legible

The unit is **x**, the height of the capital I in IronDost. Keep at least 1x clear on every side of any lockup: no text, edges or other logos inside it.

Colour

### Laundry-day blues, one warm highlight

Ink and royal carry the name, sky is the water, and sun is kept for the moments that should jump out: offers, a "new" badge, the iron's steam button. Click a swatch's button to copy its hex.

**Ink** #0E1E3D

RGB 14 30 61 Text, outlines, dark surfaces and the app sidebar.

**Royal** #1F4FD1

RGB 31 79 209 Primary buttons, links and the word "Dost".

**Sky** #3FB8EA

RGB 63 184 234 Water, fills and accents on dark backgrounds. Never text on light.

**Ocean** #1A73B0

RGB 26 115 176 Sky's partner for text on light backgrounds, such as the tagline.

**Sun** #FFC93C

RGB 255 201 60 Offers, badges and highlights. Always with ink text on top.

**Mist** #E6EEF8

RGB 230 238 248 Panels, cards and soft section backgrounds.

#### How much of each

White and mist 60% Ink 20% Royal 12% Sky 6% Sun 2%

#### Text contrast, labelled like a care tag

The dots work like the iron symbol on a clothing label: more dots, more heat it can take. Ratios follow WCAG 2.2.

| Sample | Text on background | Ratio | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aa | Ink on white | 16.5 : 1 | AAA, any size |
| Aa | Ink on mist | 14.1 : 1 | AAA, any size |
| Aa | Ink on sun | 10.8 : 1 | AAA, any size |
| Aa | Sky on ink | 7.3 : 1 | AAA, any size |
| Aa | White on royal, royal on white | 6.8 : 1 | AA, any size |
| Aa | Ocean on white | 5.1 : 1 | AA, any size |
| Aa | Sky on white, white on sky | 2.3 : 1 | Decoration only |
| Aa | Sun on white | 1.5 : 1 | Decoration only |

Three dots: 7 : 1 or more Two dots: 4.5 : 1 or more Crossed out: never for text

Voice

### Talk like the friend who already handled it

#### Friendly

Warm and direct, like a neighbour you trust with your house keys. Use people's names and the partner's name.

#### Exact

Times, prices and counts, always. "By 7:30 pm" beats "soon"; "6 shirts" beats "your items".

#### Calm

When something goes wrong, say what happened, what we are doing and the new time. No drama, no blame.

#### Local

Hinglish in ads and social posts. Plain English inside the apps until the Hindi version ships.

Pickup reminder

WriteRamesh will pick up your clothes tomorrow between 8 and 10 am.

AvoidYour pickup request has been successfully scheduled.

Running late

WriteWe're running 30 minutes late. Your clothes will reach you by 7:30 pm.

AvoidDue to unforeseen circumstances, your delivery has been delayed.

Prices

WriteSee the price of every item before you book. Pay by UPI or cash when we deliver.

AvoidUnbeatable prices starting at just ₹10!* T&C apply.

Misuse

### Six ways to break the logo

Every one of these happens on real printed bags and signboards. Send the vendor the SVG from this page instead.

![](image omitted)

Don't stretch or squash it. Scale by the corner, keeping the proportions.

![](image omitted)

Don't recolour it. Use one of the four colourways as supplied.

![](image omitted)

Don't tilt or rotate it, even for a "dynamic" layout.

![](image omitted)

Don't add shadows, glows, bevels or outlines.

![](image omitted)

Don't put the colour logo on royal, sky or busy photos. Use reverse or one-colour white.

![](image omitted) IronDost

Don't retype the name in another font. Always use the supplied files.

The zip mirrors `packages/brand` in the laundry-v2 repository. Use SVG wherever the software allows it, and PNG only where it doesn't.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|

Developers: the logo is drawn in code, so change it in `scripts/build-svg.py` and rebuild instead of editing the SVGs.

```
OUTFIT_DIR=/path/to/outfit-ttfs pnpm --filter @laundry/brand build
```
