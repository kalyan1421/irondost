## Budget

₹5,000 a month (print ₹1,500, ads ₹3,000, reserve ₹500) or ₹10,000 (print ₹2,000, ads ₹6,000, creator credits ₹1,500, reserve ₹500). ₹25,000 only after a channel shows a cost per delivered customer below the three-month contribution. Split: 70% on what works, 20% on the next channel, 10% on one experiment.
