# Irondost Clip Assets

211 images collected from the Irondost repo, de-duplicated, and renamed for video editing.

## Naming

    type_subject_orientation_WxH.ext

- type: logo, icon, social, scene, item, service, illus, screen, post, story, carousel, campaign, qr, store, banner, promo
- orientation: vertical (9:16), portrait (4:5), square, landscape (16:9 and wider), vector (SVG)

Search your editor's media bin for `_vertical_` when cutting 9:16 reels, `_landscape_` for 16:9.

## What each folder is for

- **01_Brand**: Logo bugs, intro/outro lockups, watermark. Use mono-white on dark footage, reverse on navy.
- **02_Scenes-Broll**: Background b-roll. Landscape masters for 16:9 / Ken Burns pans; vertical crops drop straight into 9:16 reels. story-steps runs in order: pile → creased → pressing → pressed → counting → packed → handover.
- **03_Garment-Cutouts**: Clean product shots on plain ground for price callouts and item pop-ins.
- **04_Service-Icons-3D**: Service cards: ironing, wash & iron, dry cleaning, saree, carpets.
- **05_Illustrations-SVG**: Flat line icons, scale to any size for motion graphics.
- **06_App-Screens**: Phone-frame inserts for 'how to book' clips. store-* already have captions; raw-ui is bare UI.
- **07_Social-Posts**: Finished posts and stories. Use as static slides or cut into a carousel-to-reel.
- **08_Endcard-CTA**: QR codes for the final 2–3 seconds of every clip.
- **09_Store-Graphics**: Wide hero art for YouTube thumbnails and landscape end cards.
- **10_App-Banners**: Offer banners from the admin panel, good for promo clips.

## Not included
