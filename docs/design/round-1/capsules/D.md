# Direction D

## Premise (subject evidence)
Pressing removes creases by flattening cloth under heat and weight. The page is pressed the same way: one size, one weight, one block, pushed to both edges, with no raised hierarchy.

## Fonts
- TeX Gyre Heros Bold 2.004 (GUST), GUST Font License (LPPL 1.3c based; free to use and redistribute), CTAN distribution. Lacks ₹.
- Fallback: ₹ from Charis Bold (SIL OFL 1.1, family "Rs", U+20B9 only). Provisional.

## Hierarchy and type roles
One role only: Heros Bold, clamp(1.2rem, 6.1vw, 3.7rem), line height 0.98, tracking -0.025em. Order is the only hierarchy.

## Case, spacing, line rules
Sentence case as supplied; all copy runs on in one paragraph in the supplied sequence, separated by spaces only. Justified text with automatic hyphenation. Page padding 5vw.

## Colour
Canvas #F2B632. One active text colour: indigo #1B2563, 100% of text.

## Space and placement
The block fills the viewport's width and is vertically centred in at least one screen height. No margins other than the 5vw padding.

## Signature typographic relationship
Hierarchy flattened into sequence: the customer reads the whole proposition as one pressed sheet.

## Invariants / allowed variation / prohibited normalization
- Invariant: one size, one weight, one colour; justified to both edges; tight leading.
- May vary: the canvas and ink pair; the face if it stays a single plain bold sans.
- Must not: add a second weight or size to recover hierarchy; break the block into labelled sections.

## Assumptions and risks
High risk for task use: prices, windows and actions are not scannable. It tests whether a pressed, uniform voice can be a brand layer over a conventional interface. Justified text with hyphenation can open rivers at narrow widths. Contrast is 7.4:1.

## Round 2 translation
Splash, welcome and marketing surfaces only; interface screens would need a second, scannable layer that keeps this face and colour pair.
