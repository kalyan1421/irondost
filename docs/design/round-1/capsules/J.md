# Direction J

## Premise (subject evidence)
"Back at your door." The doorstep is where the service starts and ends, and the partner is a person who arrives there. The page is composed on a single central axis, like what is read at a front door: few words, in order, with space on both sides.

## Fonts
Libertinus Serif Regular (The Libertinus Project), v7.051, SIL OFL 1.1, from github.com/alerque/libertinus. Contains ₹ in Regular. Small caps and old-style figures from the font's own features. No fallback in use.

## Hierarchy and type roles
1. Headline: Regular, clamp(2.3rem, 8.2vw, 3.9rem), line height 1.12, tracking -0.005em.
2. Body: Regular 1.2rem, line height 1.6, centred, max 24em.
3. Functional lines and wordmark: small caps (font feature), tracking 0.2em; wordmark 1.25rem, tracking 0.34em; "Get started" 1.3rem.
4. Times and prices: old-style figures.

## Case, spacing, line rules
Sentence case as supplied; small caps via font feature for the wordmark, "Choose your pickup and delivery times." and "Get started". Group intervals 3.8rem; "Get started" 4.2rem below the last line. No hyphenation.

## Colour
Canvas #0D3B3B. Two active text colours: ivory #F4EEDC (primary, about 78%) and brass #C9A24A (secondary, about 22%: wordmark, "Choose your pickup and delivery times.", "Get started").

## Space and placement
Centred on one axis; main column max 24em; side padding 1.5rem; top and bottom padding clamp(4rem, 15vh, 9rem). Symmetric: no element is offset from the axis.

## Signature typographic relationship
A single centred axis with small-capital brass lines at the beginning, the question and the end.

## Invariants / allowed variation / prohibited normalization
- Invariant: centred axis; small caps for functional lines; two-colour ivory and brass; old-style figures.
- May vary: the dark canvas and the metal hue; the exact measure.
- Must not: add ornament, rules or a plaque shape; turn brass into a button colour.

## Assumptions and risks
Centred text makes price and window lists harder to scan than aligned columns; the dark canvas and brass can read as a luxury formula and may not suit an everyday service. Only Libertinus Regular carries ₹; bold and italic of the same family do not.

## Round 2 translation
Welcome and thank-you screens; confirmation; receipts and policies. Interface lists would need an aligned layout that keeps the face and colour pair.
