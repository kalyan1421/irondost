# Direction H

## Premise (subject evidence)
The purpose of the service is less effort. The best version of the app asks for very little and says one thing at a time, with space around it.

## Fonts
TeX Gyre Adventor Regular 2.004 (GUST), GUST Font License (LPPL 1.3c based; free to use and redistribute), CTAN distribution. Lacks ₹. Fallback: ₹ from Charis Regular (SIL OFL 1.1, family "Rs", U+20B9 only). Provisional.

## Hierarchy and type roles
1. Headline: Regular, clamp(1.5rem, 5.4vw, 2.4rem), line height 1.2, tracking 0.
2. Body: Regular 1rem, line height 1.5, tracking 0.02em.
3. Wordmark: 0.9rem, top left.
Only two sizes in use; no weight changes.

## Case, spacing, line rules
Sentence case as supplied. Each thought is its own section; the first section sits 34vh below the wordmark, and every later section 46vh below the one before. Grouped lines (times, prices) 0.15rem apart. Bottom padding 30vh.

## Colour
Canvas #F3F5F4. One active text colour: slate #33433F, 100% of text. No tints or opacity.

## Space and placement
Sections are inset clamp(0, 14vw, 16rem) from the left and limited to 30ch. The wordmark stays at the top-left corner. Side padding clamp(1.25rem, 6vw, 4rem).

## Signature typographic relationship
One sentence per screenful: the space between sentences is as much of the design as the sentences.

## Invariants / allowed variation / prohibited normalization
- Invariant: one colour, two sizes, large intervals between thoughts, small headline.
- May vary: the interval length (provided it stays larger than the text), canvas tint, face.
- Must not: fill the gaps with decoration, illustration or a second colour; shrink the type below body size to add more silence.

## Assumptions and risks
Requires a lot of scrolling for the full story; on a phone the first screen shows only the wordmark and one line. May read as unfinished or empty to customers who want reassurance and price at a glance. Contrast is about 9:1.

## Round 2 translation
Welcome, confirmation, and waiting states; booking screens that show one decision at a time; very restrained use of colour for the single action.
