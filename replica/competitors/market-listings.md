## Product patterns seen in their screenshots (OBSERVED unless noted)

**LaundryMate** (yellow `#FECC04` and blue `#0274BC`)
- Five bottom tabs: Home, Orders, Basket, Membership, Wallet.
- Home greets by name, shows the address chip, a membership strip ("get free pickups"), and a toggle for a faster mode ("Sprint": 15-minute pickup, 4-hour delivery, one Bengaluru area).
- Two ordering styles on Home: Standard (pick items from the catalogue) and Quick (schedule laundry without selecting items).
- Catalogue: service tiles, then audience tabs (Men, Women, Kids, Household), rows with name, price and an Add/stepper. Example prices on screen: shirt ₹25 to ₹30, jeans ₹50.
- Booking shows a four-step progress strip (address, slots, basket summary, payment), a date strip, and 2.5-hour pickup windows, some tagged "+20% express fees". Delivery slot states a usual 24-hour processing time.
- Membership ("ClubUltimate"): from ₹149 (CLAIMED, Play text), guaranteed slots, automatic order creation, scheduled pickup days, no delivery charge, monthly cashback. The Breezz tier shows up to 7 pickups a week and a ₹1,000 refundable deposit.
- Promises (CLAIMED): 24-hour turnaround, free reprocessing "no questions asked", prices matching neighbourhood stores.

**LaundroKart** (cream `#F6F3EE`, plum `#2B1331`, magenta `#B8227D`, gold italic accents)
- Editorial look: serif italic accent words in headings, soft cream background, magenta-to-purple wallet card.
- Home shows the active order as a card with a progress line and "Back by <date>", a "Book again" row of recent orders, and "What can we clean?" tiles.
- Booking: where we pick up, what can we clean, when (date chips and windows such as 10:00–14:00), then **"How fast do you need it?" with Normal, Semi and Express options** showing a price multiplier.
- Order detail has a four-stage tracker (picked up, in process, ready, delivered) with timestamps, plus the bill.
- "LK Cash" wallet with recharge packs and bonus credit (screenshot: ₹10k pack with ₹2,000 bonus, ₹5k with ₹500, ₹1k with ₹50).
- Orders split into Active, Upcoming and History tabs. Services and pricing list shows "from" prices per piece.

## What customers complain about (paraphrased; see the pages for the full reviews)

- LaundryMate, Play: an item charged for but returned unprocessed; the express fee on top of a ₹50 delivery charge; pickups rescheduled at the last minute; late evening deliveries. [Play reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.laundrymate.consumer&hl=en_IN&gl=IN)
- LaundryMate, App Store: an update that crashed the app on launch; an earlier bug that hid the payment button; extra charge for premium packaging; edits made in the app not honoured by delivery. [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/laundrymate/id1632839902)
- LaundroKart, Play: missed pickups and unreachable support, though the newest of these reviews on the page are from 2022 and earlier. [Play reviews](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.laundrokart.laundrokartapp&hl=en_IN&gl=IN)
- LaundroKart, App Store: a misplaced garment with a ₹500 compensation offer (January 2023). [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/laundrokart/id1552864492)

These are single reviews, a handful per page. They show what kinds of problems happen, not how often. Star ratings per review were not shown in the page text, so no review CSV was built for these two apps.

## Limits

Only the first few reviews on each page were read. Prices shown in screenshots are sample data and may be old. Nothing was installed, nothing was signed in to, and Install buttons were not touched.
