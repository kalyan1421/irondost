### Spacing rhythm (low confidence, eyeballed)
- Roughly an 8pt grid; screen gutters about 16pt; service-grid tiles about 8-10pt apart, about 75-80pt square; slot chips about 34pt tall and 8-10pt apart; drawer rows about 48-50pt; Orders rows about 64-72pt; docked CTA bar about 48pt full width; date strip about 56pt.
- Web: 4-column card grid, about 1000px container, 24px gutters, generous section padding (80-100px) with large empty areas.

### Shape language
- App tiles: small radius (about 4-6pt) with dark 1px borders, no shadow. Slot chips: radius about 6-8pt, light 1px border. Docked CTAs: full-width flat rectangles (no radius) sitting under the safe area. Inline buttons (Reschedule, Cancel, Go Back To Home): radius about 3-4pt filled teal. Invite friends: about 8pt. Copy chip: full pill. No bottom sheets or modals observed.
- Elevation: app is flat (borders and tints instead of shadows). Website uses a soft diffuse shadow with a faint mint glow on cards and buttons. Scheduler page: single bordered card (radius about 8px, hairline border), pill Continue button.
- Dividers: 1px hairlines in drawer and Orders list.

### Texture and material feel
- Flat vector, light, "clean laundry" feel. No grain or patterns. Subtle decorative bubbles on the hero banner. Splash uses a flat illustration of folded towels and an upright iron with sparkles and steam.
- Imagery: stock photography (laundry basket, sneakers in a machine, shirt on hanger) mixed with duotone teal/green flat illustrations (scooter rider, phone with tick, rider holding box). No 3D, no mascot. The porthole-with-wave brand icon is the only recurring brand graphic.
- Icons: service-grid icons are dark outline line icons (about 1.5px stroke, rounded) with small accent details; Home tiles use teal outline icons; drawer icons are solid filled teal; bottom-tab icons are thin line. Mixed line and filled styles in one app.
- Motion hints: pager dots on onboarding and the home banner imply swipe carousels; website hero carousel has arrow controls. No other motion observed.
- Dark mode: not observed (all 13 app frames are light).

### Tone of voice (UI copy)
- Three adjectives: plain, reassuring, transactional.
- Paraphrased examples: "pick your services and a time, then relax, it is that simple" (Home); "your pickup person is on the way, have the items ready" (confirmation); "your location is not serviceable right now, log in to see the nearest areas" (gate). Short labels seen: Schedule Pickup, Accept & Proceed, Refer & Earn, Invite friends.
- Website copy is long, SEO-driven, repetitive ("Best Laundry in Hyderabad"), and makes broad claims (never damaged, guaranteed reimbursement).

### Design critique (5 bullets)
1. **Trustworthy but generic.** Mint-teal plus white is the laundry category default; the logo is distinctive but the shell is a vendor template shared with sibling brands (same developer lists Tumbledry, FABO and others). Feels budget-functional, not premium, despite "Premium" copy.
2. **Low contrast and tiny type.** White labels on mid-teal bars, pale disabled CTAs and 9-11pt labels would fail comfortable-reading tests on a budget Android phone.
3. **Schedule screen wastes space and hides meaning.** Three slot chips float on a big grey void; "Same Day + 100%" and "Next Day + 50%" are unlabeled radios with no explanation of what is being priced.
4. **No price in the booking path.** None of the 13 app frames shows a price, item count or total; trust depends on a store-side bill after intake, and reviews show bill shock. The public website price list is far clearer than the app.
5. **Website feels cluttered.** Long SEO paragraphs, repeated twin CTAs (Schedule Free Pickup and Chat on WhatsApp) every screen, three floating buttons, five testimonials all dated 26/12/2023 (stale proof) and a hero Quick Schedule widget that defaults to Bengaluru regardless of the visitor.

## E. Core flow

**Happy path (app).** The sequence is INFERRED from screens that are OBSERVED individually; no one walkthrough of the current UI was available.

1. Install and open. Intro carousel (3 slides, Skip or Next). Slide 1 OBSERVED; others INFERRED. Taps: 1 (Skip) or 2 (Next).
2. Location is checked before login (OBSERVED gate). OS permission prompt not observed (INFERRED). Taps: 1.
3. If the area is not covered the app stops at a "not serviceable, log in to see nearest areas" screen with one Login button (OBSERVED). Login is mobile number + OTP (OBSERVED on the web booking page; INFERRED for the app because a review says the app fails to send OTPs). Taps: about 3 plus 10 digits and OTP digits.
4. Home: pick one or more service tiles (Dry Cleaning, Wash and Fold, Wash N Iron, Steam Press Only...). "Schedule Pickup" is disabled until a tile is chosen (INFERRED from disabled look). Taps: 1 to 3.
5. Tap "Schedule Pickup" (OBSERVED button). Tap: 1. A grid screen with "Accept & Proceed" exists (OBSERVED) but where it sits in the path is not known. Tap: 0 to 1.
6. Address: not observed in the current UI. The legacy UI had an address card with "Select Address". Reviews report GPS picking a wrong house number and a bug that deleted a saved address. Taps: 0 to 3.
7. Schedule: date (1), one-hour slot (1), optional Same Day or Next Day radio (0 to 1), "Schedule Pickup" (1). Taps: 3 to 4.
8. Confirmation: "Please keep your items ready" with Reschedule, Cancel (OBSERVED). Taps: 0.
9. Rider pickup, intake tagging, invoice, payment request, delivery (CLAIMED on the Hyderabad page). Delivery 48 to 72 h, 24 h express (CLAIMED); reviews report delays from 5 days up to a month.

**Tap count.** Returning, logged-in user in a covered area: 4 to 5 taps (service tile, Schedule Pickup, [Accept & Proceed], slot, Schedule Pickup). **Open questions answered**

- **Serviceability / pin code:** location-based in the app; store-locator by area name, or GPS, on the website. No pin-code field was observed. Store codes embed the pincode (e.g. FAB-DILSUKHNAGAR-500060).

## H. Strengths and weaknesses vs a new hyperlocal entrant

**Strengths (evidence)**
1. **Physical footprint near the launch area.** A store 3.3 km from LB Nagar, 17 claimed Hyderabad stores, walk-in alternative that avoids the minimum (https://fabrico.in/stores, https://fabrico.in/help).
2. **Transparent public price book.** Per-store, per-garment list of 213 items with an update timestamp, and ironing priced separately (Dilsukh Nagar steam press 15-25 for common wear). A rare level of price disclosure in this category (https://fabrico.in/pricing?store=FAB-DILSUKHNAGAR-500060).
3. **Very short booking path.** Service tile, slot, confirm; no cart, no item entry, 4 to 5 taps for a returning user (Play and App Store screenshots).
4. **Breadth and long tail.** Shoes, bags, carpets, sofa, strollers, vehicle covers; Seitz chemicals and garment-tag claims; many contact channels (WhatsApp tab, call, form) (https://fabrico.in/pricing, https://fabrico.in/help).
5. **Brand recognition and SEO coverage.** 350+ stores claimed, 2.1K iOS ratings, 1L+ Android installs, localities including LB Nagar and Kothapet listed on the Hyderabad page.

**Weaknesses (evidence)**
1. **Reliability.** In the 41 Play reviews read (newest 40 plus one older), 28 are 1 star, 2 are 2 stars, 1 is 3 stars and 10 are 5 stars; in the 10 Apple reviews, 7 are 1 star. Recurring themes are missed or late pickups, deliveries of 10 days to a month, no status updates (see reviews.csv).
2. **Price opacity at booking.** No price, item count or total in any app frame; two reviews allege the billed per-kg rate was higher than quoted (reviews.csv, 2026-06-10 and 2025-10-24).
3. **App stability and UX.** OTP not arriving, stuck on location, slot booking failing, address deleted or wrong GPS pin, "UI weird and not user friendly"; ratings 3.5 Play and 3.8 iOS (reviews.csv).
4. **Support.** Canned developer replies, unanswered helpline, email reportedly bouncing, store staff unable to give status (reviews.csv, 2025-12-07).
5. **Ironing is not their hero and minimums are high.** Steam press is a side column, premium press is extra, minimum order Rs 349 (about 17 shirts at Rs 20), evening-only slots in the sample, and Telugu/Hindi not evidenced in the app (web scheduler has Hindi but no Telugu). An iron-first, low-minimum, Telugu-friendly, price-at-booking entrant has a clear opening.
